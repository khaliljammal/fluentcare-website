# FluentCare writing guide

Write for independent clinic owners, practice managers and staff evaluating routine communication. Answer one practical question per page.

## Voice

Address the reader as “you” and “your team”. Use plain words, active sentences and natural contractions. Answer early, then show a concrete example. Keep paragraphs to one idea, usually one to three sentences.

Prefer “speak with patients” over “facilitate multilingual patient engagement”. Avoid revolutionary, seamless, game-changing, unlock, empower and transform healthcare. Keep headings short and informative.

## Editing budgets

- Homepage: about 300–450 main-body words.
- Product page: about 450–650 words.
- Get started: about 150–300 words.
- Practical guide: usually 600–900 words.

These are editing budgets, not SEO minimums. Stop when the reader has the answer.

## Evidence and publication

Use the verified product evidence supplied with the brief. Keep private evidence outside the public website repository. Do not infer released capabilities, pricing, availability, accuracy, clinical outcomes or provider arrangements.

Never invent customers, quotes, reviewer credentials or results. Label illustrative examples. Cite primary sources near factual claims. Research about language access does not establish a product's accuracy.

A product page must make its intended use and relevant limits clear. Follow current approved wording supplied with the brief. Use a qualified interpreter where the situation calls for one.

Use one natural primary keyword intent per page. Give each page a unique title, description and owning URL. Use helpful internal links; do not repeat exact-match phrases to meet a density target.

Use a CTA that completes a real next step. Do not promise a download or successful application unless the destination works. A contact page is a contact step.

## AI drafting contract

Inputs: audience, practical question, owning URL, primary intent, verified facts and sources, a useful original element, required links and CTA destination.

Return concise Markdown, metadata, a claim/source record and unresolved questions. Keep missing facts in the review record rather than putting placeholders on a public page.

Before publication, check that every section adds value, factual statements have evidence, links work, metadata is unique, the preview renders, and the review record is complete.
