FluentCare provides two-way AI voice translation for routine clinic front-desk conversations, such as greetings and appointment scheduling. Staff take turns speaking, hear translated replies and can use optional captions. Start with a non-patient staff exercise while current release requirements are being confirmed.

## Practise one appointment question

Imagine a receptionist and colleague trying a fictional scheduling exchange. Use an invented appointment and no patient details. Choose the second language available in your release, place the phone where both people can hear and take turns speaking.

Check the controls together: can both colleagues hear the reply, find pause and recognise when clarification is needed? Keep a note of usability questions. This is an illustrative exercise, not evidence of translation accuracy or readiness for patient use.

## Set up the team around the workflow

Owners manage clinic membership and the subscription. Front-desk staff use individual accounts and share the clinic’s minutes. Patients do not need a FluentCare account.

Choose your clinic’s interpreter escalation route before evaluating a tool. Keep the person responsible for access, staff questions and usage limits clear. Review the [product overview](/medical-translation-app/) and [clinic pricing](/pricing/) before planning access.

## Keep the conversation within clear limits

AI translation can make mistakes. Use a qualified interpreter for informed consent, complex or high-stakes decisions, unresolved uncertainty, or whenever one is requested or required. FluentCare does not connect you to human interpreters or provide medical advice.

For a team checklist covering access, language evidence, privacy and cost, read [how to evaluate translation apps for healthcare](/resources/translation-apps-for-healthcare/).

## Confirm availability and data handling

Current release testing uses non-patient information. Clinical language evaluation and provider healthcare/retention arrangements have not yet been verified for launch. Do not submit patient information until the applicable agreements and provider settings are confirmed for your clinic.

OpenAI processes audio for translation. FluentCare does not save conversation recordings or transcripts in its own application storage; provider processing and retention are separate.
