FluentCare’s published privacy policy separates application storage from provider processing. Review both before evaluating the app. Healthcare/provider arrangements and clinical language evaluation are still being verified.

## Audio and captions

Audio is sent to OpenAI to provide translation. The policy states that FluentCare does not save conversation recordings or transcripts in its own application storage. Optional on-screen captions clear when you pause or end a session.

That does not mean no provider processes audio or that provider retention is zero. Processing and retention depend on applicable agreements and settings. Verify those arrangements for your intended use.

## Account and usage information

FluentCare retains account, clinic, membership and usage information needed to operate the service and administer access and billing. These records are separate from conversation recordings and transcripts.

For the full description of data handling, read the [application privacy policy](https://fluentcare.web.app/privacy-policy/). The policy is the reference for the app; this page is a short explanation.

## Current release limits

The published policy says to use only non-patient information during release testing. Do not submit patient information until applicable agreements and settings are confirmed. We do not claim that a general product description establishes healthcare compliance or clinical accuracy.

Public app-store downloads are coming soon. Ask the team about the current release, evaluated languages and requirements before introducing the product in your clinic.

## Optional website statistics

If you choose “Allow” on this website, we send limited page and button statistics to PostHog in the US. We use a random identifier for this browser tab and fixed categories for the page, referral source and button action.

We do not send form contents, raw page URLs or query strings, conversation content, staff identifiers or clinic identifiers. We do not enable website session recordings, person profiles or automatic interaction capture. Like other network services, PostHog receives connection information such as your IP address and browser information.

Choosing “No thanks”, enabling Global Privacy Control or sending a Do Not Track preference prevents this website capture. Use “Analytics choices” in the footer to change your choice. Without permission, the website does not send these analytics events.

This website setup is separate from the app’s release status and analytics configuration. It does not establish that production app events are being delivered.
